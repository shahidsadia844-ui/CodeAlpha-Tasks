# TASK 2: Case Study Analysis

## Selected Company: Moderna, Inc.

### 1. Biotechnology Innovation / Product
Moderna ka sabse bada innovation unka **mRNA (messenger RNA) platform** hai. Traditional vaccines ki tarah weak virus body mein dalne ke bajaye, mRNA vaccine body ke cells ko khud ek harmless protein (spike protein) banana sikhati hai, jise dekh kar hamara immune system antibodies generate karta hai. Unka sabse pehla commercial product COVID-19 Vaccine (Spikevax) tha.

### 2. Applications
* **Infectious Diseases:** COVID-19, Influenza (flu), aur RSV jaisi bimariyon ke khilaf tezi se vaccines design karna.
* **Oncology (Cancer Immunotherapy):** Personalized cancer vaccines banana jo har patient ke tumor cells ke mutabiq unka immune system activate karein.
* **Rare Genetic Diseases:** Body mein missing proteins ko mRNA technology ke zariye replace karna.

### 3. Challenges
* **Stability & Delivery:** mRNA molecule bohot fragile (nazuk) hota hai aur jaldi toot jata hai. Isko safe rakhne ke liye Lipid Nanoparticles (LNP) ki delivery system design karni padi.
* **Cold Chain Logistics:** Shuruat mein in vaccines ko extremely low temperatures (-20°C se -80°C) par store karna ek bada challenge tha.
* **Public Perception:** Ek nayi genetic technology hone ki wajah se public skepticism aur regulatory approvals ka samna karna pada.

### 4. Market Impact
Moderna ne medical history ki sabse tez vaccine design ki (sirf kuch mahino mein). Is innovation se company ki market valuation billions of dollars tak pahunch gayi. Isne pharmaceutical industry ko dikhaya ke computational design aur mRNA ke zariye aane wale waqt mein kisi bhi nayi bimari ki vaccine hafton mein banayi ja sakti hai.
