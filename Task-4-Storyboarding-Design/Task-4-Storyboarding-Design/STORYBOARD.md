# Task 4: Storyboarding Design

## 🎯 Objective
To visualize a narrative/user journey by breaking down a concept into clear, sequential visual frames.

## 📖 The Concept / Storyline
**Title:** User Registration & First Purchase Flow  
*Brief Description:* This storyboard visualizes the seamless journey of a new user signing up on our platform and successfully making their first order.

## 🎨 Storyboard Frames
Here is the visual sequence of the user journey:

![Storyboard Design](assets/your-storyboard-image.png)

### 📋 Detailed Scene Breakdown
* **Frame 1:** User lands on the homepage and feels confused by too many options.
* **Frame 2:** User finds the search bar and types the required item.
* **Frame 3:** User adds the item to the cart and sees a 10% discount pop-up.
* **Frame 4:** User enters payment details securely and completes the purchase with a smile!
