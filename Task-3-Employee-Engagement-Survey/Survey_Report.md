# 📊 Task 3: Employee Engagement Survey & Analysis Report

## 🎯 Task Objective
To design a comprehensive Employee Engagement Survey (10-15 questions) using Google Forms/MS Word, gather sample responses from 5 participants, and analyze the data to create a summary report.

---

## 📝 Part 1: Survey Design (11 Questions)
The survey was structured using a 5-Point Likert Scale *(1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly Agree)* along with open-ended feedback.

### ❓ Survey Questions:
1. **Job Satisfaction:** I feel satisfied with my current role and responsibilities.
2. **Work-Life Balance:** The company supports a healthy balance between work and personal life.
3. **Growth Opportunities:** I see clear career growth and professional development pathways here.
4. **Manager Support:** My direct supervisor provides regular feedback and supports my work.
5. **Team Collaboration:** My team members collaborate effectively to achieve goals.
6. **Compensation:** I am satisfied with my current compensation and benefits package.
7. **Transparency:** Management communicates company goals and changes transparently.
8. **Tools & Resources:** I have all the necessary tools and software to do my job efficiently.
9. **Recognition:** I feel valued and recognized when I do good work.
10. **Company Culture:** I would recommend this company to my friends as a great place to work.
11. **Open Feedback:** What is the one thing we can improve to make your work experience better?

## 🔍 Part 3: Data Analysis & Key Findings

### 🌟 Key Strengths (Highest Scores)
* **Team Collaboration (Avg: 4.6/5.0):** Employees feel a strong sense of teamwork and trust within their immediate peers.
* **Manager Support & Tools (Avg: 4.0/5.0):** Managers are supportive, and employees generally have the resources needed to execute tasks.

### ⚠️ Areas for Improvement (Lowest Scores)
* **Compensation & Benefits (Avg: 2.8/5.0):** This is the lowest-scoring area, indicating dissatisfaction or market misalignment regarding pay packages.
* **Recognition & Rewards (Avg: 3.0/5.0):** Employees feel that hard work is not being formally highlighted or rewarded enough.

## 💡 Part 4: Action Plan & Recommendations
Based on the survey results of these 5 individuals, the company should focus on:

2. **Implement Peer Recognition:** Introduce a "Monthly Star Performer" program or a Slack channel dedicated to shoutouts to improve the recognition score.
3. **Structured Career Paths:** Managers should conduct quarterly performance reviews focusing on personal career development tracks.
