# TASK 1: Market Research Report

## Industry Focus: Biotechnology (Global mRNA Therapeutics & Vaccines Market)

### 1. Target Audience
* **Healthcare Providers & Hospitals:** Jo advanced treatments aur vaccines patients ko provide karte hain.
* **Governments & Global Health Organizations (WHO):** Jo pandemics se bachne ke liye bulk mein purchases karte hain.
* **Pharmaceutical Companies:** Jo biotech startups ke sath mil kar nayi dawaiyan banana chahti hain.

### 2. Competitors & Market Trends
* **Key Competitors:** Moderna, BioNTech, Pfizer, aur Sanofi.
* **Current Trends:** Artificial Intelligence (AI) ka use kar ke vaccine processing ko fast karna aur personalized cancer vaccines par research barhana.

### 3. SWOT Analysis

| Strengths (Taqat) | Weaknesses (Kamzori) |
| :--- | :--- |
| * Tezi se naye design banana (Fast development)<br>* High efficacy (Asar-andazi) rates | * Extremely low temperatures par storage ki zaroorat<br>* High manufacturing setup costs |

| Opportunities (Mawaqi) | Threats (Khatraat) |
| :--- | :--- |
| * Cancer aur genetic disease therapeutics mein growth<br>* Developing countries mein expanding access | * Strict government regulations aur approvals<br>* Competitors ke darmayan intellectual property (patent) disputes |
