# TASK 3: Laboratory/Practical Task (Virtual/Research-Based)

## Topic: Microbial Culture Research (Case Study on Yeast / Saccharomyces cerevisiae)

### 1. Introduction
Yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) aik single-celled microorganism hai jo fungus ki family se belong karta hai. Yeh biotechnology, baking, aur brewing industries mein bohot important role play karta hai. Yeh fermentation ke process ke zariye sugar ko carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) aur alcohol mein convert karta hai.

### 2. Method (Research Methodology & Growth Requirements)
Yeast culture ko virtually study karne ke liye, unki growth ke liye darjazail conditions zaroori hoti hain:
* **Nutrients:** Yeast ko grow karne ke liye carbon source (Glucose/Sugar), Nitrogen, aur minerals ki zaroorat hoti hai.
* **Temperature:** Yeast ki optimal growth $30^\circ\text{C}$ se $35^\circ\text{C}$ ke darmiyan hoti hai.
* **pH Level:** Yeh slightly acidic environment (pH 4.5 se 5.5) mein sabse behtar grow karta hai.
* **Process:** Jab sugar solution mein yeast milaya jata hai aur temperature maintain kiya jaye, toh anaerobic respiration (fermentation) shuru ho jati hai.

### 3. Results & Discussion
Yeast culture ki growth aur fermentation ke darjazail results samne aate hain:
* **Gas Production:** Fermentation ke dauran Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$) gas banti hai, jo baking mein dough (aate) ko phoolne (rise) mein madad deti hai.
* **Ethanol Production:** Industrial level per is process se bio-ethanol (alcohol) banti hai jo biofuel ke taur per use hoti hai.
* **Growth Curve:** Yeast ka cell count shuru mein slow hota hai (Lag phase), phir bohot tezi se barhta hai (Log phase), aur aakhir mein nutrients khatam hone per ruk jata hai (Stationary phase).

### 4. Conclusion
Is virtual research se yeh conclude hota hai ke Yeast aik nihayat mufeed microbe hai. Agar isko proper temperature ($30^\circ\text{C}$) aur sugar di jaye, toh yeh fermentation ka process tezi se mukammal karta hai. Yeh biotechnology mein food production aur green energy (biofuel) banane ke liye aik buniyaadi cheez hai.
