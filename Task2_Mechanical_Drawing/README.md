# Task 2: Mechanical Drawing - Machine Component (Nut-Bolt Assembly)

## Project Description
This task covers the engineering and technical drafting of a standard mechanical machine component. A Nut-Bolt Assembly was selected to demonstrate 2D geometric design, standard dimensioning practices, and multi-view projections according to engineering design standards.

## Multi-View Projections Included:
- **Front View:** Showcases the major vertical height, thread engagement area, and the cylindrical body of the bolt.
- **Top View:** Highlights the hexagonal shape of the nut and bolt head with accurate circular annotations.
- **Side View:** Details the thickness of the assembly component from the lateral profile.

## Design Workflow & Standards
1. **Geometric Representation:** The layout outlines standard projection methods to clearly separate the top, front, and side profiles.
2. **Dimensioning:** Applied standard engineering units and annotations to ensure technical accuracy for component assembly.
3. **Drafting Principles:** Clear visual lines are used to distinguish hidden geometry from visible structural outer edges.
