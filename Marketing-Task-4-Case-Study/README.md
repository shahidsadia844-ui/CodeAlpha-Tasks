# TASK 4: Case Study Analysis (Marketing Strategy)

## Selected Company: Zomato

### 1. Business Model & Marketing Techniques
Zomato ek online food delivery aur restaurant discovery platform hai. Unka business model delivery commissions, premium subscriptions (Zomato Gold), aur restaurant advertisements par chalta hai. 

#### Core Marketing Techniques:
* **Meme Marketing & Social Media:** Zomato Instagram aur Twitter par current trends aur viral memes par furan posts banata hai, jise young audience bohot pasand karti hai.
* **Push Notifications:** Zomato app users ko unke lunch ya dinner time par funny aur emotional short messages bhejta hai (e.g., "Biryani is calling, pick up the phone!").
* **SEO & Content Marketing:** Jab bhi koi Google par kisi restaurant ya dish ke baare mein search karta hai, Zomato ka review page top par aata hai.

### 2. Why the Strategy Worked & Lessons Learned
* **Hyper-Localization:** Zomato ne shehar aur local festivals ke mutabiq apni marketing ko customize kiya (jaise Cricket Match ya Eid/Diwali par special discounts aur ads).
* **Emotional Connection:** Unhone food ko sirf ek product nahi balki ek emotion (feeling) ke taur par sell kiya.

#### Lessons Learned:
1. **Consistency is Key:** Social media par rozana trending topics par active rehne se brand recall value barhti hai.
2. **Customer Retention:** Naye customers dhoondne se zyada zaroori unhe push notifications aur subscription plans ke zariye engage rakhna hai.
