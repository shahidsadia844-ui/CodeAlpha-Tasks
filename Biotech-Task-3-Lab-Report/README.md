# TASK 3: Laboratory/Practical Task (Research-Based)

## Topic: Microbial Culture Research (Yeast - Saccharomyces cerevisiae)

### 1. Introduction & Objectives
Yeast aik single-celled eukaryotic microorganism hai jo fermentation ke process ke liye biotechnology research mein bohot scale par use hota hai. Is research ka objective yeast cells ki growth requirements aur sugar metabolism ko study karna hai.

### 2. Methodology (Growth Conditions)
* **Substrate / Food:** Glucose/Sugar solution ko carbon aur energy source ke taur par select kiya gaya.
* **Temperature & pH:** Optimal growth ke liye temperature 30°C aur slightly acidic environment (pH 5.0) maintain kiya gaya.
* **Process:** Anaerobic conditions (oxygen ke bagair) mein yeast sugar ko consume karta hai.

### 3. Results & Discussion
* **Fermentation Output:** Process ke dauran Carbon Dioxide (CO2) aur Ethanol (alcohol) bante hain. Gas bubbles ki production se fermentation rate ka andaza hota hai.
* **Growth Phases:** Yeast cells shuru mein adjust hote hain (Lag phase), phir tezi se divide hote hain (Log phase), aur aakhir mein jab sugar khatam hoti hai toh growth ruk jati hai (Stationary phase).

### 4. Conclusion
Yeast culture biotechnology mein baking, bio-ethanol biofuels, aur pharmaceutical industries ka ek buniyaadi hissa hai. Sahi temperature aur carbon source milne par iska cellular metabolic rate maximum ho jata hai.
