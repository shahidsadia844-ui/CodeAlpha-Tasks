## 📜 1. Introduction

---

## 📅 2. Leave Policy
We believe in maintaining a healthy work-life balance. Employees are entitled to the following types of leaves annually:

### A. Annual/Vacation Leaves
* **Quota:** 15 paid days per calendar year.
* **Approval:** Must be requested at least 7 days in advance via email to the manager.

### B. Casual & Sick Leaves
* **Quota:** 10 paid days per year (for sudden illness or urgent personal work).
* **Notification:** Employees must inform their team lead before 9:00 AM on the day of absence.

### C. Public Holidays
* All gazetted government holidays will be observed as paid days off.

---

## 🤝 3. Code of Conduct
Our Code of Conduct defines the expectations for behavior and professionalism within the startup.

### A. Workplace Professionalism
* **Respect:** Zero tolerance for discrimination, bullying, or harassment of any kind.
* **Integrity:** Employees must maintain honesty in all financial, technical, and professional dealings.

### B. Confidentiality & Data Security
* Employees must not share any internal source code, product design, or client data outside the organization.

### C. Dress Code
* **Monday to Thursday:** Smart Casual / Professional.
* **Friday:** Casual.

---

## 🏠 4. Work-From-Home (WFH) Guidelines
To support flexibility, our startup offers hybrid working models under the following conditions:

### A. Eligibility & Frequency
* Employees can request up to **2 days of WFH per week** with prior approval from their supervisor.

### B. Expectations & Availability
* **Core Hours:** Employees must be active and available on communication channels (Slack/Teams/Email) between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
* **Internet Connection:** A stable, high-speed internet connection is mandatory to ensure smooth video calls and project updates.

### C. Productivity Standards
* WFH is not a day off. Deliverables, tasks, and sprint deadlines must be met exactly as if working from the office.
